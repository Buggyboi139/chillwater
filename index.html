Real water. Real tackle. No nonsense.

Launch your boat onto quiet mountain water and fish it your way.

Read the bottom on sonar, match real lures and rigs to sixteen freshwater species, and mount your trophies on the cabin wall.

Sixteen species

Bluegill to sturgeon, each with its own water, depth, bait and fight.

Real tackle

Rods, reels, line, lures and live bait that behave the way they do on the water.

Your boat

Motor out, find the drop-offs on sonar, and work them from the deck.

Your cabin

Sell your catch at the dock, take orders, and hang the best ones on the wall.

Chillwater Lake Fishing is a single-player game for iPhone and Android, with optional leaderboards. It plays offline, has no ads and no energy timers, and saves your progress on your phone.

Need help? See Support.
Your privacy: no account, no ads, no tracking. The game sends only your purchase history and anonymous gameplay events, and your catches if you join the leaderboards. See the privacy policy.
