Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Chillwater Lake Fishing has no ads and doesn't track you. You never make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Two things leave your device: your purchase history, so that what you buy reaches your game, and anonymous gameplay and diagnostic events that help us fix problems and improve the game. A third leaves it only if you join the leaderboards or redeem a support code: the catches and codes you send, under a random ID.

What stays on your device

Your progress, catches, tackle, boats, cabin and settings are saved on your device. We don't receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy coins or a pack, Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

To confirm purchases and add them to your game, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service that works on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • what you bought, its price and currency, and the purchase's transaction ID and date;
  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email or Apple Account);
  • basic information about the app and device, such as the app version, operating system version and store country.

RevenueCat uses this only to process purchases. Its own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Gameplay and diagnostics

The game uses PostHog, an analytics service that works on our behalf, to show us how the game is played and how well it runs. It receives:

  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier);
  • gameplay events, such as the goals of the first hour you finish, the screens you open, coins earned and spent, and purchases (what was bought and its price, never your payment details);
  • diagnostics, such as the app version, your device's model and operating system, its language and screen size, the graphics level the game settled on, how long it took to load, and errors or unexpected closes;

We've set PostHog to discard the IP address your device connects from. It sets no cookies, keeps no profile of you, and records nothing of your screen. This data isn't linked to you, and we use it only to fix problems and improve the game. It isn't used for advertising or to track you, and it isn't sold. PostHog's own privacy policy is at posthog.com/privacy.

Initial 1.0.0 builds send the same kind of information to GameAnalytics instead, which, like any server, sees the IP address of the device that connects to it. Its privacy policy is at gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Leaderboards and support codes

The leaderboards and the Fish of the Week are optional. Nothing is sent until you tap Join on the Boards screen, or redeem a code in Settings. The first time you do either, the game makes an anonymous sign-in: a random ID with no name, email or password. Our game server, hosted by Supabase in the United States, then keeps:

  • that random ID, and the sign-in's sessions, which record the IP address and device type that they came from;
  • if you join the boards, an angler name that the game picks for you (two random words, never your own name) and the title you choose to show;
  • the catches your game sends after you join: the fish, its weight and length, the rod and the bait or lure, the in-game day, hour, weather and part of the lake, and when it arrived. Other players see your angler name, title and catches on the boards;
  • your Fish of the Week entries and results;
  • the support codes you redeem, and how many wrong codes were tried in the last hour.

We use this only to run the boards and to deliver the coins a code carries. It isn't used for advertising or to track you, and it isn't sold. Supabase's own privacy policy is at supabase.com/privacy.

To delete it, open Boards, then You, and tap Leave the boards. That removes your angler name, catches, entries, results and the sign-in itself from the server. What you won stays in your game. If you redeemed a code without joining the boards, write to us and we'll delete the sign-in.

Nothing else

The game shows no advertising, doesn't use your device's advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. If any of this changes, we'll update this policy first.

Children

The game isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. The only such information is the purchase record held by RevenueCat, the anonymous events described under Gameplay and diagnostics and, if you joined the boards or redeemed a code, the records described under Leaderboards and support codes. To ask about it, or to have it deleted, write to support@chillwaterfishing.com.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
