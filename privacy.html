Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Chillwater Lake Fishing is a single-player game. It has no ads and doesn't track you. You never make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. The only information that leaves your iPhone is your purchase history, so that what you buy reaches your game.

What stays on your device

Your progress, catches, tackle, boats, cabin and settings are saved on your iPhone. We don't receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy coins or a pack, Apple (the App Store) handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

To confirm purchases and add them to your game, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service that works on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • what you bought, its price and currency, and the purchase's transaction ID and date;
  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email or Apple Account);
  • basic information about the app and device, such as the app version, iOS version and store country.

RevenueCat uses this only to process purchases. Its own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Nothing else

The game doesn't connect to any server of ours. It collects no analytics or crash reports, shows no advertising, doesn't use your device's advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. If any of this changes, for example if online features are added, we'll update this policy first.

Children

The game isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. The only such information is the purchase record held by RevenueCat. To ask about it, or to have it deleted, write to support@pearltide.world.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
