Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Chillwater Lake Fishing is a single-player game. It has no ads and doesn't track you. You never make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Only two things leave your device: your purchase history, so that what you buy reaches your game, and anonymous gameplay and diagnostic events that help us fix problems and improve the game.

What stays on your device

Your progress, catches, tackle, boats, cabin and settings are saved on your device. We don't receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy coins or a pack, Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

To confirm purchases and add them to your game, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service that works on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • what you bought, its price and currency, and the purchase's transaction ID and date;
  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email or Apple Account);
  • basic information about the app and device, such as the app version, operating system version and store country.

RevenueCat uses this only to process purchases. Its own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Gameplay and diagnostics

The game uses GameAnalytics, an analytics service that works on our behalf, to show us how the game is played and how well it runs. It receives:

  • a random ID that the app creates on your device (not your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier);
  • gameplay events, such as the goals of the first hour you finish, the screens you open, coins earned and spent, and purchases (what was bought and its price, never your payment details);
  • diagnostics, such as the app version, operating system, screen size, the graphics level the game settled on, how long it took to load, and errors or unexpected closes;
  • your approximate country, from your connection's IP address, which GameAnalytics doesn't store.

This data isn't linked to you, and we use it only to fix problems and improve the game. It isn't used for advertising or to track you, and it isn't sold. GameAnalytics' own privacy policy is at gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Nothing else

The game doesn't connect to any server of ours. It shows no advertising, doesn't use your device's advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. If any of this changes, for example if online features are added, we'll update this policy first.

Children

The game isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. The only such information is the purchase record held by RevenueCat and the anonymous events held by GameAnalytics. To ask about it, or to have it deleted, write to support@chillwaterfishing.com.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
