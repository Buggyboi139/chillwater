Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to us and include "Chillwater" in the subject.

Email: support@pearltide.world
We answer within a few days. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened.

A purchase didn't arrive

Coins and packs are added as soon as the App Store confirms the payment. If one didn't arrive, close the game completely and open it again, with an internet connection. If it still hasn't arrived, open the Store in the game (the + beside your coins) and tap Restore purchases.

Restoring purchases on a new iPhone

The First-Time Angler Pack, All Rods & Reels and Full Fleet are yours for good. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Store and tap Restore purchases. Coin packs are used up when you spend them, so they can't be restored.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds for the App Store. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved on your iPhone, not on our servers. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it can't be recovered. Purchases that last (the packs above) come back with Restore purchases.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game plays offline. You need a connection only to buy something or to restore purchases.

Deleting your data

The game keeps nothing about you on a server. The only record is the purchase history described in the privacy policy. To ask about it, write to the address above.
